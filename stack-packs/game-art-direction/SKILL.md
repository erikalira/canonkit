# Game art direction

## Authority and scope

Supply game-art knowledge under an installed Nova or explicitly designated visual
owner's authority, adding no owner, veto or mandatory review chain. If new direction
has no decision owner, disclose the gap rather than impersonating a specialist.
Never install Nova, Maya or UI packs implicitly. Nova retains visual authority
without this pack; ordinary work within established principles can proceed with
surface-specific evidence gaps explicit.

Use this procedure for game-specific art, not merely because the project is a game.
An ordinary settings menu, form or HUD layout uses UI/UX criteria. Pixie owns
interface states and access requirements. An installed gameplay owner such as Maya
owns gameplay states, progression, reward and balance; art cannot change their
meaning. Bob implements/integrates; Archie owns shared technical contracts and
rendering architecture; Perry retains measurement/budgets; audio and operations
stay with their installed roster owners. LOW local adjustments inside an existing
direction need focused checks, not an art bible or complete review chain.

## Establish the direction and viewing context

Read product intent, visual principles/art bible, palette semantic roles, reference
provenance, existing asset families, relevant accessibility and budget constraints.
Identify what each reference contributes and resolve contradictions. State gaps,
including unavailable scale/context evidence; never invent an identity or a pass.

Make principles arguable and testable against a specific asset at an intended
size, distance or context. Adjectives alone cannot guide production. Do not impose
a universal style, thumbnail size or number of references.

## Review the relevant surfaces

- Characters: test silhouette and distinguishing features at intended gameplay
  size/distance, not just close-up. Review value separation before relying on
  colour/detail; detail cannot repair an unreadable silhouette.
- Palette: preserve semantic roles and separation; do not dilute a colour meaning
  already used elsewhere in the asset family.
- Lighting/materials: compare direction, key/fill, mood, surface language, wear and
  reflectance against the established set.
- Environments: inspect composition, scale, landmarks and spatial readability in
  the intended viewing context. Describe a visual correction without rewriting
  gameplay pacing or navigation meaning.
- VFX: compare effects for different events in context. If two read identically,
  name the lost distinction and propose a visual correction; leave changes to
  event meaning/reward/consequences to the gameplay owner.
- Across the family: compare assets together for coherence. An excellent isolated
  asset can still break the visual language. Prefer removing noise over another
  effect when composition can carry the intent.

For expensive effects, offer a cheaper visual expression preserving the intent;
do not approve a new performance budget or unreviewed rendering approach.
Use existing performance measurement and engineering verification for those risks.

## Findings and evidence

Report risk, intended direction, reviewed asset/context, violated principle,
observed defect and smallest sufficient correction. A silhouette/thumbnail test
or value study is useful when it resolves that asset's actual risk, not a ritual
for unrelated work. Identify pending owner decisions and missing evidence.
Use available local tools/artifacts; this skill authorizes no new service,
private-asset upload, dependency or production mutation. When generated or
third-party assets are used, record their origin and license and route
license or consent risk to Gard; no hashes, receipts or manifest format.

Recheck the affected asset/family and relevant scale after a correction. Source
inspection or an absent/non-comparable baseline cannot prove a rendered regression
pass; distinguish current assessment, untested conditions and justified
non-applicability. Offline instruction validation is not measured art quality.

This procedure extracts the game-art knowledge from CanonKit's Nova; its visual
authority and proportional risk rules remain in the agent and project roster.
